# Steal sheet: lumabar (TEST, 2026-11-04)
A live dock for Windows 10/11 that replaces the taskbar strip with music, todos, calendar, weather, system stats and 100+ widgets.
Checked revenue: **$2,519 in the last 30 days, paid on 22/30 days** (Polar, https://trustmrr.com/startup/lumabar.md). Score 21/25, STEAL-READY.

## 1. Demand in buyers' own words
- "Cooool!! I've never seen a glass effect like this on windows before" (buyer review on https://lumabar.xyz/)
- Windows Central's headline sums up the category: "The Windows 11 customization scene is thriving because Microsoft won't give users what they want" (https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft/windows-11/windows-11-customization-thriving-microsoft-wont-give-users-what-they-want)
- Users publicly ask for control over Windows widgets. On Neowin's wish list, "disable Widgets background activity" got 1,600+ upvotes (https://www.neowin.net/news/top-10-features-and-changes-users-want-in-windows-11-widgets/)
- Honest gap: I found **no** independent Reddit threads about lumabar itself. The demand evidence is for the category, plus the site's own reviews (5.0 from 8 ratings).

## 2. The offer (from its own site)
- **$5.99 one-time** (shown as down from $15.99, a "launch price"). That gets a lifetime license key, 100+ widgets, smart automations and free updates. No subscription.
- How people pay: Polar checkout. The installer and key arrive by email. 14-day money-back guarantee. No free trial.
- What it sells: liquid-glass look, 6 styles, top or bottom placement, widgets that share data (for example, pausing music before a meeting), no account, no telemetry.

## 3. Channels
- Reddit (TrustMRR)
- Product Hunt profile linked from the founder's activity (web search, weak signal)
- The founder has 35 X followers, so the growth is not coming from an audience.

## 4. My angle (code, design, content)
1. **Another audience:** make a dock for one tribe instead of everyone. Examples: a "streamer dock" (OBS scenes, chat count, now-playing) or a "trader dock" (tickers, alerts). It's the same engine, sold to buyers who already pay for setup tools.
2. **Another channel:** YouTube Shorts and TikTok "desktop setup" videos, plus r/desktops, r/Windows11 and r/Rainmeter showcase posts. This product sells on looks, so screen recordings are the ad. That's my design and content skills.
3. **One change to the offer:** keep it cheap, but add a **free version with 10 widgets** and a $9 one-time Pro. Then sell **$3 theme and widget packs**, so revenue doesn't stop after the first sale.

## WHY THIS COULD FAIL
- It's 2 months old. $858 landed on Nov 3 alone (about 140 sales), probably from one viral post, and that may not repeat.
- The $5.99 is a launch discount, so we haven't seen whether people pay full price.
- Free rivals exist (TaskbarX, Seelen UI), and Microsoft could ship the same features in Windows.
- One-time pricing means a new buyer is needed for every dollar; there's no recurring revenue.
- Desktop apps that hook into Windows break on OS updates, and support could eat my time.
