## 2. The offer (from its own site, checked 2026-11-05)
- **$5.99 once** ("Launch price · 63% off", shown down from $15.99). Lifetime license key, every update included, no subscription.
- How people pay: Polar checkout. The installer and key arrive by email. 14-day money-back guarantee, refunded through Polar.

## 4. My angle (code, design, content)
1. **Another audience:** streamers. **Already built as OnAir Dock** (`onair-dock/`): OBS live/REC status, scene switching, now playing, BRB timer, run of show.
2. **Another channel:** screen-recording Shorts and TikToks of the bar in action, plus showcase posts in streamer communities on Reddit. This product sells on looks.
3. **One change to the offer:** free core version plus a $9 one-time Pro (done in OnAir Dock). Next step: $3 widget or theme packs, so revenue doesn't stop after the first sale.

## WHY THIS COULD FAIL
- It's only 2 months old. $858 on Nov 3 (about 143 sales) looks like one viral post, and that may not repeat.
- $5.99 is a launch discount, so full-price demand is unproven.
- Free rivals exist (TaskbarX, Seelen UI), and Microsoft could ship the same thing.
- One-time pricing needs a new buyer for every dollar.
- Windows updates can break desktop docks, and support eats time.
