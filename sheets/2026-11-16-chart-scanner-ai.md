# Steal sheet: Chart Scanner AI (2026-11-16)
An iPhone app: screenshot a trading chart (or search a symbol like AAPL or BTC-USD) and get an AI **Long / Hold / Short** reading with the reasons, plus market news and short lessons.
Checked revenue: **$3,588 in the last 30 days, paid on 30/30 days**, 114 active subscriptions, $2,981 MRR (RevenueCat, https://trustmrr.com/startup/chart-scanner-ai.md). Score **20/25, STEAL-READY**.
Monthly: Sep $0 → Oct $1,826 → Nov $1,770 by the 16th. The biggest single day is only 7% of the month.

## 1. Demand in buyers' own words
- Thin. The App Store shows **5.0 from only 1 rating** (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chart-scanner-ai/id6813120341), so I found no buyer quotes to copy.
- The category itself is proven: the App Store has dozens of near-identical apps ("Chart AI – Trading Scanner", "ChartScan: AI Chart Analysis", "TradeAI: Stock Chart Scanner"). One competitor's own reviews complain about "the lack of analysis results" (Chart AI, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chart-ai/id6636484876).
- The real demand signal is 114 people paying weekly or half-yearly, every single day for 30 days.

## 2. The offer
- From its own site (https://chartscannerai.com/): free download; screenshot or symbol → Long/Hold/Short + "the why"; daily market news; stocks, indexes and crypto; a learning section (Elliott waves, patterns). Big disclaimer: "not investment advice".
- Prices from its App Store listing (the site shows none): **Premium $7.99** and **Premium $49.99** in-app purchases. The founder's TrustMRR text says $7.99/week and $44.99 for 6 months.
- How people pay: Apple in-app subscription (RevenueCat).

## 3. Channels
- TikTok (TrustMRR)
- App Store search for "chart AI" and "chart scanner" (web search: the copycat flood shows that's where buyers look)
- Founder has 0 X followers, so growth isn't audience-driven.

## 4. My angle (code, design, content)
1. **Another audience:** the same "photo → instant AI reading" engine for something safer than trading, such as **sports-betting-free fantasy stats**, **crypto tax screenshots** or **fitness/nutrition labels**. Same hook, no financial-advice risk.
2. **Another channel:** short videos of "I screenshot this chart → AI said Short → here's what happened". Your content skill fits, and TikTok is already their only channel.
3. **One change to the offer:** drop the $7.99/week trap for **$5/month or $39/year**, and lead with *learning* ("explain this chart to me like I'm new") over *signals*. That means fewer refunds, fewer complaints and less regulatory risk.

## WHY THIS COULD FAIL
- Trading apps sit next to "financial advice". App Store review or regulators can pull them, and one bad call can bring angry reviews.
- The category is flooded with near-identical apps; standing out costs ad money.
- Weekly subscriptions churn fast, and most apps like this live on paid TikTok/Meta ads, which you'd have to fund.
- Only 1 public rating, so buyer satisfaction is unproven.
- AI "readings" can be confidently wrong, and users lose real money.
