What each bottom line includes (which costs are taken out):

• SaaS Platforms – profit before tax: all expenses are taken out (staff, marketing, travel, development, hosting, admin and card processing fees). Tax is not taken out.

• GoldenMark Money: only the 2¢ cost per check and digital marketing are taken out. Staff, hosting and admin are not added yet.

• ZSI PAY – gross fee revenue: the full fee we earn, because no expenses are added yet.

• Miscellaneous – net after development cost: project income minus the 50% development cost.

Combined bottom line is an upper bound (the best case). Because some costs are still missing, the real profit will be somewhat lower.
