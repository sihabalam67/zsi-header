Revenue model. $75 per month ($900 per year) plus a $500 setup fee, so a new subscription's first full 12 months are worth $1,400. The 5 platforms share one sales, marketing and technical team.

¹ Hosting assumes $5 per active subscription per month, 7% of subscription revenue. AI-heavy platforms may cost more. Development and maintenance is set at 33.3% of setup-fee revenue each year.

Main assumption to validate: acquisition efficiency. Sales and marketing per new subscription moves from $560 in Year 1 to $259 in Year 5, which requires referrals, a repeatable sales process and effective digital campaigns.

Excluded unless covered by the budgets above: founder compensation, sales commissions, financing costs and taxes. Startup funding needs require a monthly cash-flow forecast, because expenses can occur before customer payments arrive. Conservative and Growth scenarios are illustrative variations (new subscriptions ×0.7 with 15% cancellations, and ×1.25 with 8% cancellations).
