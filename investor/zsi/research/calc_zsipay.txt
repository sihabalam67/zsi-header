How revenue is earned. ZSI PAY charges 0.5% of online sales processed, which is $5.00 for every $1,000. Only online sales processed through ZSI PAY count.

Assumptions. Restaurants process $20,000 and e-commerce businesses $30,000 in online sales a month, constant over time. Merchants join evenly through each year, and none leave.

Gross fee revenue. These figures are before operating expenses. They assume ZSI PAY keeps the full fee, with processor and card-network charges paid separately. If those charges come out of the fee, enter them in the optional setting to see retained revenue.
