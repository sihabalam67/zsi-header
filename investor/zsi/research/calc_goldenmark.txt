Pricing. Customers pay $0.65 per digital check with no monthly subscription or setup fee. Each check costs $0.02 to produce, leaving $0.63 (96.9% gross margin) before marketing and other costs.

Not net profit. The remaining amount must also cover any staff, software maintenance, hosting, customer support, payment processing, administration and taxes not already included in the per-check cost.

Customers required. Year 1 needs about 100 average active customers printing 100 checks a month; Year 5 needs about 2,000 printing 125. Revenue grows at 124% a year on average over the period. Increase marketing only when campaigns bring in customers whose check usage supports the cost.
