# Coin Theory Uploader: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Coin Theory Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Coin Theory YouTube channel
to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

## What the app accesses
When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to:
- upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's YouTube channel, and edit their settings;
- read the channel's YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, audience retention), read-only.

## How the data is used
This access is used only to publish Coin Theory's own videos and to measure how they perform so
future videos can be improved. The app does not access other channels, viewers' personal data,
email, contacts or any other Google services.

## Storage and sharing
Sign-in tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private work environment. No data
is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## Revoking access
The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
