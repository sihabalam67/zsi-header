Paste everything below the line into ChatGPT (agent mode). The owner logs in to Canva themselves.

You are helping me (the owner of the faceless YouTube money channel "Coin Theory") collect stock video clips in my Canva account. Claude will later export these designs and edit the clips into my videos, so the names and layout must be EXACTLY as described.

## Rules (follow all of them)
1. Use only Canva's own stock videos (Elements > Videos). My Canva Teams plan covers Pro clips. Never buy anything, never upload files, never use anything that costs money or credits.
2. No logos, brand names, readable store names or app names, and no famous or recognizable real people. Ordinary people are fine; prefer hands, objects and places.
3. No clips with text, captions, watermarks or "subscribe" graphics baked in.
4. No sad, violent, sexual or scary clips. Bright, clean, modern, real footage (not cartoons, not AI-looking).
5. Clip length 5 to 30 seconds. Sharp (HD or better).
6. Shape: for "Vertical" designs pick tall (portrait) clips when available; for "Horizontal" designs pick wide clips.
7. ONE clip per page. On every page: drag the clip onto the page, then right-click it and choose "Set video as background", so it fills the whole page edge to edge. Add nothing else: no text, no music, no stickers, no transitions.
8. Every clip in a design must be different. Don't reuse the same clip across designs.
9. Do not edit, move, rename or delete any of my other designs. Do not share or publish anything.
10. If a search term gives nothing good, use the closest similar search and say which one you used in your final report.

## Where
Put every new design inside my Canva folder named "Coin Theory B-roll" (it already exists; search for it under Projects). If you can't find it, create a folder with exactly that name.

## How to make each design
- Vertical: Create a design > Custom size > 1080 x 1920 px.
- Horizontal: Create a design > Custom size > 1920 x 1080 px.
- Rename the design to the exact name given below (click the title at the top).
- Add pages (the "+" below the page) until there is one page per search term, in the order listed.
- Fill each page with a clip for that search term, as in rule 7.
- Canva saves automatically; wait until it says saved before moving on.
