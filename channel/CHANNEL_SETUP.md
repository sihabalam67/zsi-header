# Create the Coin Theory channel (about 10 minutes)

## 1. Create it (on youtube.com, signed in to your Google account)
1. Profile picture (top right) -> **Settings** -> **Add or manage your channel(s)** -> **Create a channel**.
   This makes a **Brand Account**: you can add helpers later, and it stays separate from your gaming channel.
2. Name: **Coin Theory**
3. Handle: try these in order: `@cointheory`, `@cointheoryhq`, `@cointheoryexplained`, `@thecointheory`

## 2. Branding (YouTube Studio -> Customization)
- **Profile picture:** `brand_profile.png`
- **Banner:** `brand_banner.png` (text sits inside YouTube's safe area for phones and TVs)
- **Video watermark:** `brand_watermark.png`, display "Entire video"

## 3. Description (Customization -> Basic info)
> Why do prices keep rising? Why are houses so expensive? Where does your money actually go?
>
> Coin Theory explains money and the economy with short animated stories and real data from official
> sources like the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Census Bureau and the Federal Reserve. Every number on
> screen is sourced in the description.
>
> Hosted by Penny, a coin who's seen a lot of prices change.
>
> New videos every week. Education only, not financial advice.

**Links:** none needed yet.

## 4. Channel settings (Studio -> Settings -> Channel)
- **Country of residence:** where you actually live (must match your future AdSense account)
- **Keywords:** `economics, money, inflation, personal finance, housing market, interest rates, economy explained, animated explainer, finance education, cost of living`
- **Advanced settings -> Audience:** "No, set this channel as not made for kids"

## 5. Verify your phone (Studio -> Settings -> Channel -> Feature eligibility)
Unlocks custom thumbnails, videos over 15 minutes and live streaming. Required before the first upload,
or your thumbnails won't work.

## 6. Upload defaults (Studio -> Settings -> Upload defaults), saves time on every upload
- Category: **Education**
- Language: **English**
- Caption certification: "This content has never aired on television in the U.S."
- Description footer (Basic info -> Description): `Education only, not financial advice.`

## 7. Then
Follow `WEEK1.md`, starting Monday with `ep01_inflation.mp4`.
