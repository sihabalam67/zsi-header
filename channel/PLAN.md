# Coin Theory: channel plan

**What it is:** animated explainers about money and the economy, using real data and an
original mascot, Penny the coin. One clear question per video, answered with charts
built from official sources (BLS, Federal Reserve/FRED, World Bank, Census).

**Audience:** English-speaking adults 18-40, US/UK first (highest ad rates).
Videos get dubbed audio tracks later for reach.

**Format**
- Long video: 6-10 min, 1 per week. Hook in the first 15 s, chapters, real data on screen.
- Shorts: 2-3 per long video (vertical cut of the best moment).
- Narrator: Kokoro `af_heart` voice, free and licensed for commercial use. Swap in your
  own recorded voice any time; the pipeline supports it.
- Every video ends with "education only, not financial advice" and lists its sources.

**Brand**
- Name: Coin Theory. Check that the @handle is free when you create the channel.
- Colours: deep navy background, gold (Penny), mint = good, coral = bad, sky blue = data.
- Fonts: Poppins (on screen), Anton/Bebas Neue (thumbnails).
- Thumbnail rule: max 4 words, one number, Penny's face, high contrast.

## Weekly workflow
1. Claude proposes the script with sources, and you fact-check it (about 5 min).
2. Claude renders the video, thumbnails (A/B), title, description, chapters and tags.
3. You upload, tick "altered or synthetic content" (AI voice), set the A/B thumbnail test.
4. After 7 days, share YouTube Studio screenshots (retention, CTR) so the next episode improves.
