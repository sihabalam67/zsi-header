# Growth ideas beyond the daily routine (Nov 25, 2026)
Rules: free, no fake engagement, no logins, real sourced numbers, originality gate.

## 1. Ride the calendar (seasonal search spikes) - make the Short/video 1-3 days BEFORE the day
| Date | Moment | Angle (verify data first) |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 25 | Thanksgiving | DONE: AFBF dinner for 10 = $58.63 Short |
| Nov 27 | Black Friday | are the "deals" real? CPI/price history of a TV, toy, sneakers |
| Dec 1 | Cyber Monday / card debt | holiday shopping on a 22% APR card: what a $500 gift really costs (FRED TERMCBCCINTNS) |
| mid Dec | Holiday spending | NRF holiday spending per person; gift budget story |
| Dec 31 | New Year resolutions | "save $X a week in 2027" money story (originality: new plot) |
| Jan-Apr | Tax season | refund size (IRS filing season stats), "where your tax dollar goes" |
| monthly | CPI release day | same-day "what got more expensive" Short (BLS) |
Add each to the topic-scout brief two weeks ahead.

## 2. Answer what people type into YouTube (free autocomplete mining)
`https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&ds=yt&q=<seed>` (no key). Nov 25 seeds found:
"why is everything so expensive now / in america", "how to save money with low income / on groceries / as a teenager",
"what if you invested 1000 in the s&p 500", "how much money do you need to retire / live comfortably / quit your job",
"costco thanksgiving dinner 2026". Use the exact phrase in the first line of the description and as a tag; build
evergreen search-first videos from the strongest (they keep getting views for years, unlike news).
Done: Grandpa story description now opens with "What if you invested $1,000 in the S&P 500 in 1996".
