# Story bible: keep every story different (YouTube "inauthentic content" policy)

YouTube does not monetize channels whose videos feel interchangeable, quote (Nov 24, 2026 policy page): "Videos
where characters are put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome (i.e., using a highly
similar storyline template across multiple videos)". Our risk audit (channel/research/channel-risk-cointheoryhq.html)
scored 60/100 because stories 1-3 share one plot. Rule: each new story differs from the last 3 in EVERY column.

Plot types still unused (pick one the last 3 did not use): true-history heist or crime (Newton vs the
counterfeiter), scam/con unmasked, comedy of errors where the "smart" plan fails, a business rise-and-fall,
historical fall (Mansa Musa crashes gold prices), a riddle/game show, a time-travel "what would $X buy" tour, a
cautionary tale where saving alone was not enough (e.g. hidden fees, inflation, a scam), a true viral moment
(the 10,000-bitcoin pizza).
Endings must vary too: twist, bittersweet, comedic failure, open question, true historical outcome.
Never reuse: the "same money, different choices" setup, the 3-path contest, "Time does the heavy lifting", the
crash-receipt device, the same closing lesson.
