## s04 pitch: "What If You Could See Everyone's Real Savings?" (format 4: superpower POV story)
Inspired by the format of ConChaser's "What If You Could See Everyone's Future Wealth?" (2.46M views, 23x); our own
story, our own twist: every number the glasses show is REAL (Fed SHED emergency savings, Fed SCF medians by age,
NY Fed household debt, BLS). Penny narrates; "you" are the hero (second person); ~12 min.
Suspense engine: a rule, a ticking clock and a number we refuse to show until the end.
- 0:00 Cold open: "You put on the glasses. Every person on the street has a number over their head. One of them is
  red." A man in a brand-new truck: the number is NEGATIVE and falling. Question: what is the red number?
- The rules (0:40): a pawn-shop pair of glasses; the shop owner: "Never look in a mirror." (ticking clock: why?)
- Ch1 "Everyone's real savings": the street as a data map. The flashy couple, the quiet bus driver with a huge
  green number, a student. Each reveal = one real statistic (e.g. how many adults could cover a surprise $400
  bill with cash). Twist: rich-LOOKING and rich are not the same.
- Ch2 "The numbers move": you discover the numbers tick in real time: a swipe at a shop makes one drop, a paycheck
  makes one jump, and one keeps falling while its owner sleeps (card interest). Re-hook: "Then I saw a number
  falling faster than anyone's. It belonged to someone I love."
- Ch3 "The person I love": your sister / best friend's number is falling; you have to tell her without sounding
  crazy. Conflict, comedy, and the real math of a credit card balance.
- Ch4 "The bus driver's secret": the quiet man with the big green number explains how (one habit, real data);
  he also wore glasses like these once.
- Ch5 "The mirror": the ticking clock pays off. You finally look. We never show the number on screen: you see
  your own face react. Ending (new type: open/bittersweet): you take the glasses off; the numbers vanish, but you
  know yours can change. Comment prompt: "What would the number over YOUR head say? (You don't have to say it.)"
Different from s01-s03: no inheritance, no contest, no "same money two paths"; POV hero; open ending.
Thumbnail (child test): you (a cartoon kid/young adult) shocked, a homeless-looking or plain man with a huge green
number, a flashy man in a sports car with a red negative number. 3 words max, e.g. "WHO IS RICH?".
