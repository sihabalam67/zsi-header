# ep08: Midterms and your money (long, Mon Nov 2) + Short

Data: Shiller S&P 500 monthly (channel/data/shiller_ie_data.xls, Yale), price only. Window: October of the midterm
year (the month before the vote) to October a year later. 1950-2018: 18/18 up, avg +18.4%, min +3.0% (2010),
max +30.9% (1954). Other years avg +5.6%, 18 of 54 down. Midterm years Jan->Oct avg +0.3%, down 9 of 19.
2022: Jan->Oct -18.5%; Oct 2022 -> Sep 2023 +21.2% (dataset ends Sep 2023). Capital Group/BNY: no negative year
after a midterm since 1950. Nov->Nov window: 1986 ~flat (fine print). Everything is checked in ep08_midterms.py.
