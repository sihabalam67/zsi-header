# Board brief: The Barber Shop (working name)

## The idea, in the founder's words
"The Barber Shop where I kill the barbershop." Price: free for one month, then if they like it, $10 a month.

## What it is
A done-for-you website business aimed first at barbershops. The founder builds a finished website for a specific shop *before* contacting the owner, using public data about the shop (name, phone, address, hours, Google rating and reviews, booking link if they have one). The owner is then shown their own ready site and offered it free for the first month, then $10/month.

## Who pays
Owners of small independent barbershops, plus similar local shops (nail salons, hair salons, dry cleaners, electricians, plumbers and others). Many have no website or only a social page or a booking-app listing.

## What the customer gets
A hosted, mobile-friendly site: services and prices, hours that highlight today, a booking button that opens their existing booking system (Setmore, Square, Vagaro, etc.) or calls them, Google rating and review quotes, map link, FAQ, a sticky mobile call bar, and structured data for Google. Sites stay hidden from Google until the owner approves.

## What already exists
- A site engine with 297 business-category templates (212 trade profiles).
- 171 sample templates with real-quality generated photos, live at nyc-shopfronts.vercel.app.
- 271 real North Carolina shop sites built and live (hidden from search): about 50 barber, 67 hair, 79 nail, the rest other trades.
- Lead lists: 4,937 local businesses across Manhattan, Florida, Texas, New Orleans/Corpus Christi, Phoenix/Las Vegas, North Carolina, Nashville/Chattanooga and Boise.
- Hosting on Vercel.

## Not yet known
- Budget, and whether the founder works on this full-time.
- How owners are contacted and who sells (cold call, text, walk-in, email).
- How payment is collected and what happens to the site if an owner stops paying.
- Real conversion rates, churn, or cost to acquire a paying shop.
- Whether any owner has yet seen or paid for a site.
